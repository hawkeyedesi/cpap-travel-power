How to test your setup before you fly

Find out at home, not on night one in a new place, whether your chain really survives a power cut. Here's the plan we're following.

1. Start full

Charge the CPAP battery to 100% and note how it's connected (charger, cables, adapter). Test the exact chain you'll travel with.

2. Awake "wall-yank" tests (2–3 times)

  1. Set up the full night chain: wall → charger → CPAP battery → AirSense 11.
  2. Start therapy with the mask on, sitting up and awake.
  3. Pull the charger out of the wall (or switch off the socket).
  4. Watch and feel: does the CPAP reboot, show an error, or pause airflow even for a moment? Does the battery show it's now discharging?
  5. Plug it back in and check it returns to pass-through and charging.

Do it two or three times. A setup that passes once and fails once isn't good enough.

3. A dry overnight on battery alone

With the humidifier and heated tube off, sleep a whole night on the CPAP battery with the wall unplugged. In the morning, note how many hours you got and how much charge is left. That's your real number for your pressure and mask.

4. Time the recharge

  • Time a full recharge from the wall charger.
  • Time a recharge from your power bank too (for us, the EcoFlow over USB-C) and note how much of the power bank it used. That tells you how many "rescue" top-ups you really have.

What to write down

  • Pressure setting and mask type
  • Did the CPAP blip or reboot on any wall-yank?
  • Hours on battery overnight, and % left
  • Recharge time: wall, and from power bank
